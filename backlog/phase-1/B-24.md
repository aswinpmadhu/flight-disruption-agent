# [services] RebookingService (B-24)

**Phase: P1 · Wave 1 · Milestone cell: M2 · Priority P0 · Unassigned.**
**Blocked by:** B-06, B-22, B-01c. **Blocks:** B-25, B-37. **Effort:** 1.75 h (planning estimate; the claimant replaces it). **Class:** SC.
**Prep:** B-01c Protocol; B-06 and B-22 interface blocks.
**Scope (FR-15, FR-24, docs/03 §5.6, §6.4, §6.5):** `rebook(session_id, pnr, itinerary_key)` looks up the approval in the store **keyed by the session**, validates PNR and itinerary, **re-checks seats (`SEATS_UNAVAILABLE`)**, consumes the approval and swaps the booking **atomically under a lock**; otherwise it returns a rejection and changes nothing. An approval whose granting turn has ended returns `APPROVAL_EXPIRED`. **No approval parameter, no payment.** The service has **no access to search-consent records.** It records the consume event in the trace.
**Interface (fixed here):** `RebookingService(world, store)` implementing the `rebook` Protocol.
**Files:** `flight_agent/services/rebooking.py`, `tests/unit/test_rebooking_service.py`.
**Acceptance criteria:** a direct call without approval is rejected; the key is compared as an **exact string** and never parsed; two keys differing only in a date, or in segment order, give `WRONG_FLIGHT` (see the DK10 block below).

**References:** FR-15, FR-24, NFR-08; docs/03 §5.6, §6.4, §6.5.
