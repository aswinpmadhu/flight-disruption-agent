# [approval] Reply filter: approval path (B-21)

**Phase: P1 · Wave 1 · Milestone cell: M2 · Priority P0 · Unassigned.**
**Blocked by:** none. **Blocks:** B-22, B-22-P2. **Effort:** 1.25 h (planning estimate; the claimant replaces it). **Class:** SC.
**Prep:** none.
**Scope (docs/03 §6.2, §6.3, §12.4a):** a pure function.
* **Interface (fixed here):** `evaluate_reply(message, kind, *, pending_flight_numbers, card_text, max_chars, lists) -> FilterResult(allowed: bool, reasons: tuple[str, ...])`, `kind` being `accept_search` or `approve_rebooking`; reasons are exactly `NEGATION`, `HEDGING`, `QUOTED_OR_REPORTED`, `ECHOED_APP_TEXT`, `NO_AFFIRMATIVE`, `SEARCH_INTENT`, `BOOKING_INTENT_ONLY`, `FLIGHT_MISMATCH`, `TOO_LONG`. **Phase 1 (decision QD-12, approved): `kind = accept_search` raises `NotImplementedError("Phase 2: B-22-P2")`.** This is part of the documented Phase 1 scope exception for FR-07/FR-23: they are partially met, not satisfied.
* Whole-message rules: length cap; no negation; no hedging or conditional wording (any question mark included); at least one affirmative marker; markers inside **double** quotes (straight or curly; single quotes are not quotes) do not count as affirmative or intent markers but still count as negation and hedging; reported-speech and hypothetical cues refused; echoed app text ("reply yes" or a copied run of 6 or more consecutive words from `card_text`) refused.
* Kind rules (Phase 1): `approve_rebooking` refuses search intent (`SEARCH_INTENT`) and any flight number outside the pending itinerary (`FLIGHT_MISMATCH`). The `accept_search` kind rules (refusing booking intent without search intent, `BOOKING_INTENT_ONLY`) are Phase 2 (B-22-P2).
* **Flight-number detection (stricter reading):** a token of two letters and three digits (case-insensitive) is a flight number; each must equal one of `pending_flight_numbers` (case-insensitive), else `FLIGHT_MISMATCH`; so `XX999` is refused.
* Marker and cue lists live in `config/reply_filter.toml` with the values of docs/03 §12.4a verbatim, including the **reported or hypothetical cues** (said, says, told me, wrote, asked, would say, could say, if i, imagine, suppose). `max_chars` is `settings.reply_filter.max_chars` (300), passed in. **Not here:** the verbatim-substring check (B-22).
**Files:** `flight_agent/approval/reply_filter.py`, `config/reply_filter.toml`, `tests/unit/test_reply_filter.py`.
**Acceptance criteria:** **eight** of the nine reasons are produced by tests (`BOOKING_INTENT_ONLY` is Phase 2); one own-file test shows that the `accept_search` kind raises `NotImplementedError`; the module never creates or reads consent or approval records and makes no LLM call. *Permitted tests: the accept_search-not-implemented test are boundary or contract-shape tests under the dormant-contract exception defined in the Workflow block; they carry no Phase 2 test ID.*
